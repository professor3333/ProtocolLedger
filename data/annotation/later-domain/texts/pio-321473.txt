Western and dot blot
Lyse cells in RIPA Buffer supplemented with protease adn phosphatase inhibitors. For a 6-well plate, use 50-100uL of RIPA. If the solution is too viscous, add more RIPA as necessary
Centrifuge samples at 15000 x g, 4 °C, 10 minutes to pellet cell debris and insoluble proteins
Determine protein concentration by Pierce BCA protein Assay
Prepare samples with 20μg of protein, 1x LDS sample buffer (Invitrogen NP0007), 1x reducing agent (BioRad 1610792), and RIPA buffer to a total volume of 20uL (or maximum for gel size)
Load samples into a 4-12% Bis-Tris. Include one lane with a protein ladder
Run the blot at 60mV for 00:20:00 and then 120mV for 01:00:00 or just before the loading dye runs off the bottom
Transfer the gels to a PVDF membrane using teh BioRad TransBlot Turbo Transfer System with the standard protocol (00:30:00 ; Up to 1.0A; 25V)
Fix the blot in 4% paraformaldehyde for 00:40:00 This step is optional but improves the blot quality for a-synuclein and phsophorylated a-synuclein
