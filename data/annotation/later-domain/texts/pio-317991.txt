Genomic DNA extraction from Wahlenbergia (Bluebell) leaves
Aliquot a required volume of lysis buffer for your samples. We use 800 µL of lysis buffer per sample, consisting of ~100-200 mg of ground leaf tissue.
Allow samples to cool to room temperature.
Add 1x volume of chloroform:IAA (24:1) and mix thoroughly for 10-15 seconds.
Centrifuge samples for 10 minutes at 20,000 rcf.
Centrifuge with 20,000 rcf for 30 min at 4 °C.
Add 750 µL of 70-80% ethanol and mix by inversion.
Resuspend DNA pellet in nuclease-free water or Tris (10 mM Tris-Cl, pH 8).
Prepare lysis buffer.
