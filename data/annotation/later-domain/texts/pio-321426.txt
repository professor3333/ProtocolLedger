Inducing a-synuclein aggregation with PFFs in 2D and 3D culture
Thaw a-synuclein PFFs immediately before use
Add PFFs to complete media in a microcentrifuge tube to a final concentration of 4μg/mL. Also prepare a microcentrifuge tube with media and no PFFs
Remove 90% of the media, removing as much as possible without drying out the cells
Sonicate PFFs (and no PFF control) in a water bath sonicator with the program: 30 seconds on; 30 seconds off; repeat for 10 cycles
Replace with complete media with freshly sonnicated PFFs
Continue with half media changes every 3-4 days for at least 2 weeks
