Swift cDNAtag Seq Protocol (bench-top)
Quantify extracted RNA using the Qubit RNA High Sensitivity Kit per the manufacturer's protocol. Retain all quant values — these are required for the cDNA pooling step.
Add 2 µL of 30 µM BC_N9RT primers to 4 µL of RNA sample (total = 6 µL per well). Mix at 1600 rpm on plate shaker for 1 min, then spin down. Place sample plate on ice.
Prepare RT Buffer Mix on ice (per reaction): Reagent Volume (µL) 10 mM dNTP Mix 1 5X SSIV Buffer 4 100 mM DTT 2 Total 7
Add 7 µL RT Buffer Mix to each sample (total = 13 µL). Mix at 1600 rpm for 1 min, then spin down
RNA Fragmentation Fragment RNA on thermocycler at 94°C. NOTE: Empirically determine fragmentation time for your sample type and desired library fragment size. Recommended starting point: 6–10 minutes. CAUTION: Hot start required — place samples on thermocycler only after it reaches 94°C, then close lid and start the timer. Remove plate immediately after time is complete. Plate is HOT. Immediately place on ice for 2 min or until cool to touch, then spin down. Ensure thermocycler cools to 4°C before proceeding.
Prepare RT Enzyme Mix on ice (per reaction): Nuclease-free H₂O 3 SuperScript IV Reverse Transcriptase 1 Qiagen RNase Inhibitor 1 Total 5
Add 5 µL RT Enzyme Mix to each sample (total = 18 µL). Mix at 1600 rpm for 1 min, then spin down
Run reverse transcription on thermocycler: Temperature Time 25°C 10 min 55°C 30 min 80°C 10 min 4°C Hold
