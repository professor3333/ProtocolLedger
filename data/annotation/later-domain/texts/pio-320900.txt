Glyoxal Based Transcardiac Perfusion, Cryopreservation, and Immunohistochemistry Protocol for Mouse Brain Tissue
In a fume hood add 50 mL of type I water to a glass volumetric flask (100 mL).
Slowly add 22.5 mL of 40% glyoxal solution to the volumetric flask containg type I water, using a 25 mL pipette.
Homogenize the solution, by gently swirl the volumetric flask
Slowly add 8 mL of glacial acetic acid (99%) to the mixture, by using a 10 mL pipette.
Add to the solution NaOH pellets to adjust the pH to 4-5. After addition of NaOH gently swirl the solution until homogenization.
Bring the final volume to 100 mL with type I water, and homogenise by gently swirling the flask.
Prep & collect all materials in advance [transport tray, waste bags, labelled Falcon tubes 15 mL for brain post-fixation, labelled Falcon tubes 50 mL for PBS 1x and glyoxal solution, syringes, needles, ice, dissection kit, isoflurane, anaesthesia chamber, animal/user/ORBEA identification paperwork].
Transport the animals to the procedure room using an appropriate transport container.
