Simplified Agrobacterium-mediated transformation of Botrytis cinerea using mycelia
Induction medium, IM Prepare IM by supplementing MM base to the following final concentrations: 40 mmol/L MES 5 mmol/L D-glucose 0.5% glycerol 50 µg/mL thiamine, add immediately before use 200 µmol/L acetosyringone, add immediately before use
Solid induction medium Prepare solid IM using the same formulation as IM, with the addition of: 2.5% w/v agar Prepare solid IM fresh before each transformation.
Minimal medium base, MM base Prepare MM base with the following final concentrations: 11.8 mmol/L K₂HPO₄ 10.7 mmol/L KH₂PO₄ 2 mmol/L MgSO₄ 3.8 mmol/L (NH₄)₂SO₄ 2.6 mmol/L NaCl 9 µmol/L FeSO₄ 2.3 mmol/L CaCl₂ 1% w/v sucrose Autoclave MM base medium using the standard liquid cycle and cool before adding heat-sensitive supplements or antibiotics.
Transfer an actively growing B. cinerea colony onto PDA containing 25 µg/mL streptomycin using a sterile 1 cm cork borer and incubate the plate at room temperature, approximately 23 °C, for 3-5 days. On the day of transformation: Gently scrape mycelia from the agar surface using a sterile scalpel Transfer the collected mycelia to a sterile 2 mL microcentrifuge tube Wash the mycelia twice with sterile deionized water Centrifuge at 5000 rpm for 5 min at room temperature Resuspend approximately 50 mg wet weight of pelleted mycelia in 200 µL sterile deionized water. Use the mycelial suspension fresh on the same day.
Streak A. tumefaciens C58C1/pCH32 carrying pFLexpress or a pFLexpress derivative onto LB agar containing 50 µg/mL kanamycin and 5 µg/mL tetracycline. Incubate at 28 °C for 48 h. Pick a single A. tumefaciens colony and Inoculate into 5 mL MM broth, incubate at 28 °C, 250 rpm, for 48 h. On the day of transformation, transfer 200 µL of the 48 h MM culture into 5 mL IM broth and incubate at 28 °C with shaking until the culture reaches OD600 ≥ 0.25.
In a sterile 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tube, combine: 150 µL induced A. tumefaciens culture in IM broth, OD600 ≥ 0.25 150 µL B. cinerea mycelial suspension Incubate the mixture at room temperature, approximately 23 °C, for 30 min. Spread 250 µL of the co-cultivation mixture onto freshly prepared solid IM containing 50 µg/mL thiamine and 200 µM acetosyringone. Incubate plates at room temperature, approximately 23 °C, for 3 days to allow T-DNA transfer from A. tumefaciens to B. cinerea.
Once hygromycin-resistant colonies emerge, transfer 1 cm agar plugs from resistant fungal growth to fresh PDA containing 250 µg/mL hygromycin B. Incubate at room temperature, approximately 23 °C, until fungal growth is visible Repeat transfer to fresh PDA containing 250 µg/mL hygromycin B for a total of three rounds of hygromycin selection. After three rounds, maintain purified transformants on PDA with appropriate selection
After 3 days of co-cultivation, identify an area of the solid IM plate containing visible fungal growth. Using a sterile cork borer, excise an approximately 1 cm² agar section containing fungal growth and transfer the agar section to PDA containing 200 µg/mL cefotaxime, 250 µg/mL carbenicillin, and 250 µg/mL hygromycin B. Incubate at room temperature, approximately 23 °C, for 5–10 days and monitor for emergence of hygromycin-resistant fungal colonies.
