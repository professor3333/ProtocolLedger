Multiplexed Iterative FISH Experimental Protocol SOP002.v4.8
Part 1 of this protocol describes the steps to setup an iterative RNA FISH experiment for tissue or cell-based samples. These steps are focused on the biochemistry for tissue or cell preparation, encoding probe hybridization (labeling), sample gel embedding and clearing of the sample.  **All reagents for this protocol should be prepared sterile and RNase-free. **All incubation periods should be done protected from light. **Reagent/buffer preparation instructions follow this protocol below. **Safety note:  This protocol uses formamide which is a teratogen and can cause developmental malformation.  Always work in a fume hood with formamide to avoid inhalation and avoid physical contact.
Refer to current version of SOP003 for protocol on Coverslip Functionalization. PDL-coated coverslips are preferred as tissue can be post-fixed to this surface using 4 % volume, PFA
To permeabilize the tissue, immerse the slip mounted tissue in 70 % (v/v), ethanol and nuclease free water Overnight at 4 °C (recommended) in a Pyrex 60 mm petri dish (Fisher 08-747A).
Rinse the sample 3-4x for 00:05:00 in 1x PBS solution. Aspirate PBS rinse from the sample.
To permeabilize cells, immerse the slip mounted sample in 70 % (v/v), ethanol Overnight at 4 °C in a 60 mm Pyrex petri dish (Fisher 08-747A). Alternatively: 1. pipette 100 µL, permeabilization buffer (PBS-t) to each well and incubate at Room temperature for 00:10:00 with gentle rocking. 2. Rinse with permeabilization buffer rinse (PBS-tw) and rinse again with 1x PBS. 3. Aspirate off most of the PBS rinse and move on to step 15.
With the sample immersed in 1x PBS, using a hydrophobic pen, draw a barrier around your sample anddry the barrier at 37 °C for00:05:00 min. Ensure sample does not fully desiccate.
Assemble humidified chamber (see fig. 1 of the .pdf version of this protocol) and preheat at 50-55 °C .
Dispense 125 µL, 47°C prewarmed Encoding Hybridization Buffer containing 5-200 micromolar (µM), encoding probes and 1-5 micromolar (µM), linker. Add a RNase-pretreated overlay coverslip to reduce evaporation.  Parafilm the petri dish and place in the humidified chamber.           **Adjust the probe concentration depending on the sample x-y size, and sample thickness. For encoding probes, also consider the total probe number with a concentration of 1nM/encoding probe for a 10µM sample thickness.
