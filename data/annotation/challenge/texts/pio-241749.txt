Preparation of Lung Tissue Blocks for MPLEx
Place the sample in the cryostat (allow to thermally equilibrate ~20 min)
Cut 2x sections (50 μm) and place sections in pre-weighed 2 mL Sorenson MµlTI™ SafeSeal™ Microcentrifuge Tubes VWR International (Avantor) Catalog #53550
Use a Rapid-Core Sampling Punch - 4 mm Ted Pella Inc. Catalog #15115-10 to punch 2 tissue cores from each tissue section and place the punches in pre-weighed 2 mL Sorenson MµlTI™ SafeSeal™ Microcentrifuge Tubes VWR International (Avantor) Catalog #53550 *Note: When punching the tissue cores, avoid regions with OCT, as this can be difficult to remove from the sample and may yield contamination within the mass spectrum.
